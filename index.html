I'm a Controls & Software Engineer with 6+ years delivering end-to-end automation solutions at an ISO 13485 / FDA-registered contract manufacturer. My track record centers on shipping complete systems — from the PLC sequence and electrical panel to the ONNX inference server and Azure dashboard — that replace manual labor and recurring downtime with quantifiable gains.

I work fluently across the full automation stack: Allen-Bradley ControlLogix & CompactLogix (Studio 5000), Siemens TIA Portal / WinCC, ISA-88 machine architectures, Keyence / Cognex / Omron vision, YOLO / ONNX models running on NVIDIA GPUs, C# REST services on Azure, and AutoCAD Electrical schematics. Whether it's a lights-out press cell, a 99%-accurate defect detector, or an 8-second automated cycle that eliminated a full operator shift — I deliver the complete solution.

Prior to Auer Precision I designed power and controls systems for induction heating equipment at Ajax Tocco Magnethermic, and hold an M.S. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (GPA 4.0) from Youngstown State University.
